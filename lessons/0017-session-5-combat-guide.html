Session 5 Prep · Party analysis · Book 1 · Session log

Combat thesis: misfortune is a choice of target, not weather. Unluck, the snare, and fake surrender ask whether the cohort answers fear with slaughter or with the care they practiced when Anchor talked pugwampis down — and whether Kurshkin’s “purge” story survives contact with how they fight.

Battle plan — who does what

KurshkinStay in Unluck Aura range, out of melee. Shortbow the summoner / backline. Escape script below 16 / 10 HP.
HavvixRush melee. Break bones. Fearful of small metal bells (frightened 1 within 30 ft). Retreat <6 HP.
PugwampisClutter melee, force aura saves, skirmish with bows. Flee <6 HP (barn habit). Recognition optional.
Aura order matters. Succeeding vs any pugwampi Unluck Aura (even DC 16) grants 24-hour immunity to all pugwampi unluck auras — including Kurshkin’s DC 20. Do not let a lone add rush ahead and gift easy saves. Open with Kurshkin’s aura overlapping the party, or keep adds stacked with her so the first save is against DC 20.
  • Round 0: Heroes approach slick grass / snare zone. Perception DC 18 for the snare. Gremlins on or under stage.
  • Round 1: Kurshkin bows from ~15–20 ft of as many PCs as possible. Havvix closes. Pugwampis spread so auras cover eidolons + MODU + PCs without giving free DC 16 saves first.
  • Priority targets: Tiny/backline summoners (drop summoner → lose eidolon). Cy’An / MODU if clustered. Do not waste all shots on the beefiest eidolon.
  • Bells: Cascade cold-iron / small metal bells → Havvix frightened 1 within 30 ft. Kurshkin ignores superstition. Telegraph the jangle.
  • Do not Elite Kurshkin mid-fight unless the pad still melts after two full rounds of adds. Action count > HP bumps.

Kurshkin

Ancient pugwampi jinxer. Comedy-menace voice; genuine grudge. Mistook barn clear + fey ward as another purge. Prefers living victims to corpses — cowardice dressed as cruelty.

How to run her
  • Open Stealth +11 if surprise fits; otherwise bow from aura range. Stay adjacent to cover / stage edge, not in a PC’s face.
  • If pressed into melee: drop bow, shortsword, lean on Lucky Stabs vs anyone who failed Unluck.
  • <16 HP: tactical retreat — Stride toward tunnels / understage / grass, Shoot or Raise a Shield fictionally as she goes.
  • <10 HP: fake surrender (Deception +8). When they drop guard, spider sting (touch) then run. Ch 3 wants the escape option — do not force a coup de grâce.
  • Gust of wind: clear a flee lane, knock a blocker prone, or punish a clustered melee. Save it for the escape round if possible.
  • Collateral Damage: keep a pugwampi or Havvix adjacent when heroes Swing wild — crit misses become friendly fire comedy.
Spell sheet — cast at the table

Kurshkin innate values: DC 20, spell attack +10. (Havvix innate DC 17 / +9 if you need them.)

Spider Sting Spell 1

Once · 2 actions · touch · Fortitude

You magically duplicate a spider’s venomous sting. You deal 1d4 piercing damage to the touched creature and afflict it with spider venom. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

  • Critical Success The target is unaffected.
  • Success The target takes 1d4 poison damage.
  • Failure The target is afflicted with spider venom at stage 1.
  • Critical Failure The target is afflicted with spider venom at stage 2.

Spider Venom (poison); Level 1; Maximum Duration 4 rounds. Stage 1: 1d4 poison damage and enfeebled 1 (1 round); Stage 2: 1d4 poison damage and enfeebled 2 (1 round).

Escape trick: fake surrender → touch sting → run. Needs adjacency.

Gust of Wind Spell 1

Once · 2 actions · 60-foot line · Fortitude · duration until start of her next turn

A violent wind issues forth from your palm, blowing from the point where you cast to the line’s opposite end. Extinguishes small non-magical fires, disperses fog and mist, blows objects of light Bulk or less, and pushes larger objects. Large or smaller creatures in the area must attempt a Fortitude save. Creatures that later move into the gust must attempt the save on entering.

  • Critical Success Unaffected.
  • Success Can’t move against the wind.
  • Failure Knocked prone. If flying, take critical failure instead.
  • Critical Failure Pushed 30 feet in the wind’s direction, knocked prone, and take 2d6 bludgeoning damage.

Speak with Animals Spell 2

At will · usually out of combat

Allows conversation with animals. Irrelevant unless an animal companion / wild animal is present; eidolons are not animals for Unluck exclusion either — they still save vs aura. Pugwampi adds also have this at will.

Prestidigitation Cantrip

Flavor / sabotage garnish

Minor sensory tricks, soil/clean, move 1 Bulk of material, etc. Use for mean comedy (mud in eyes fictionally with GM fiat only if the cantrip covers it) — do not invent damage. Havvix and pugwampis have this too.

Destructive Smash A vexgit’s maul Strike against an object ignores up to 5 of the object’s Hardness.
Gremlin Snare 3 (conjuration, primal) Frequency once per hour; Effect The vexgit creates a snare of their level or lower that normally takes 1 minute to craft at no cost. A single vexgit can maintain one gremlin snare at a time — if they create a new snare, the previously created one vanishes. (Already used before the fight for the hampering snare on the map.)
Rusting Grasp 2 (primal, transmutation) Frequency once per hour; Effect The vexgit makes a +9 spell attack against a creature or unattended object. On a hit, deals 1d6 damage (doubled on a critical hit) to a metal item the target is wearing or holding, ignoring Hardness. An unattended metal item takes this damage automatically. If a creature uses Shield Block with a metal shield against this attack, the shield is automatically broken, but no other item is rusted on that attack.
Speedy Sabotage When a vexgit succeeds or critically succeeds at a check to Disable a Device, they gain an additional success toward disabling a complex device.
Bell superstition AP overlay Havvix is frightened 1 while within 30 feet of anyone displaying small metal bells. Kurshkin is immune to this fear.
How to run him
  • Charge the densest melee. Maul for big swings; bite when MAP bites or for agile follow-ups.
  • Rusting Grasp once: metal shield, weapon, or shiny implement — punish Shield Block especially.
  • Do not spend the fight re-crafting snares; the map snare is his contribution.
  • <6 HP: retreat. He is not loyal enough to die for Kurshkin’s grudge.
  • If bells are out: play the rattled crustacean — still vicious, but rattled and easier to Demoralize fictionally.

How to run the adds
  • Job is action clutter + aura coverage, not damage. Shortbow from flanks; shortsword only if cornered.
  • Stay near Kurshkin so Collateral Damage has a redirect target and auras overlap hers.
  • Somewhat deaf — yell to each other; Stealth is fine until they open mouths.
  • <6 HP: flee toward hatch / understage (barn habit). Lethal on fleeing gremlins is a thematic hangover, not Ot’s fury tonight.
  • If fight melts round 1: drop a fourth pugwampi from under the stage as late reinforce before Eliting Kurshkin.

Hampering Snare

Hampering Snare

Item 1 · Consumable · Mechanical · Snare · Trap · Core Rulebook 590

You arrange brambles, wires, sticky goo, or other materials to interfere with a creature’s movement. The square with this snare, as well as three adjacent squares (to form a 10-foot-by-10-foot area), become difficult terrain when the first creature enters the snare’s square. The difficult terrain affects the creature’s movement right away, including its movement into the triggering square, and it lasts for 1d4 rounds after the snare is triggered. A creature can use an Interact action to clear the difficult terrain out of a single square early.

Spot before trigger: DC 18 Perception. Tiny: gremlins do not trigger. Grass is already difficult — snare stacks as more difficult-terrain squares in the 10-ft patch (still difficult, not greater, unless something else says so).

Party AE reminder

Six level-1 PCs + two eidolons + MODU. Constructs ignore nonlethal. Unluck hits anyone who isn’t an animal / gremlin / gnoll — including constructs and eidolons. Cold iron weakness is real if anyone still carries Cascade bells or cold-iron gear.

Escape, capture, and combat-adjacent aftermath

  • Kurshkin escapes: Ch 3 return seed. Fine outcome.
  • Captured: Blames Stone Ghost. Knows almost nothing — ghostly, knows grounds, demands obedience; tunnels too small for humans; many more gremlins. +30 XP for the name. Kurshkin is the talker; Havvix is Undercommon-only.
  • Ot arrives win, lose, or flee. Not angry about lethal self-defense. Ceremony cancelled. Level 2 after the fight resolves.
