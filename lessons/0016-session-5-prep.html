Uzunjati — story of the week

Usual meeting place, morning after mail. Okoro Obiyo waves from the path; Noxolo dances in a dashiki, finger-snaps, pet “snabble” (crocodile/bear cub mash) at her ankles.

  1. Okoro frames Uzunjati: tell stories, learn stories — training for speakers and listeners.
  2. They want a story, not a checklist. Solo tales or one collaborative telling.
  3. Optional DC 15 Performance — or skip the check if the table is engaged. Other skills OK.
  4. If more than half fail / refuse to take it seriously → cohort looks like fools; friendship with Noxolo (and somewhat Okoro) cools. Okoro is more forgiving.
  5. Noxolo’s follow-up: what is the moral? Assignment incomplete without one. Let them land it; don’t supply the thesis for them.
  6. Cue: Teacher Ot wants them at the outdoor ceremony stage at dawn; wear beaded bracelets; dress nicely. Free afternoon — book allows skip to morning.

Combat pad — this table will stomp the printed budget

Table tuning Umbo (solo C3) was a Moderate-for-4 that played as Low against 5 PCs + two eidolons + MODU. Printed ceremony is Kurshkin C3 + Havvix C1 = 120 XP (Severe for 4). For six level-1 PCs, Severe wants ~180 XP. Bodies beat Elite: more turns on the monster side, not a secretly tougher Kurshkin.

Recommended add

If fight still melts round 1 Do not stack Elite mid-fight. Drop a fourth pugwampi from under the stage as a late reinforce, or let Kurshkin use the snare / aura / fake surrender fully. Action count > HP bumps.

How to play the pad

  • Diegesis: Kurshkin brought “friends who already know these initiates” — optional recognition of barn pugwampis (same faces, angrier).
  • Tactics: pugwampis clutter melee and trigger Unluck proximity; Havvix still the bone-breaker; Kurshkin bows from aura range.
  • Nonlethal: Magaambya preference still matters thematically, but Ot is not upset if they kill in self-defense. Leaving Kurshkin alive (or letting her escape) preserves Ch 3 callback — do not force a coup de grâce.
  • MODU / eidolons: constructs are immune to nonlethal; two summoners = two “drop summoner, lose two bodies” failure points — Kurshkin should sometimes shoot the Tiny/backline summoner, not only the eidolon.
  • Do not also Elite Kurshkin unless the padded fight somehow still feels trivial after two full rounds of adds.
Why not Elite-only? Elite raises AC/HP/to-hit. It does not fix “7 bodies vs 2.” Umbo proved that. Pads are the lesson from Session 3 — apply them here before rewriting the AP.

Aftermath — Ot, Stone Ghost seed, level 2, Study homework

  1. Ot arrives whether PCs win, lose, or flee. Not angry about lethal force in an ambush.
  2. Interrogation: captured gremlin blames Stone Ghost. Havvix = Undercommon only; Kurshkin is the talker. They know almost nothing: ghostly nature, knows the grounds, demands obedience; tunnels too small for humans; many more gremlins than anyone realizes. +30 XP if they learn the name.
  3. Ceremony cancelled (shock / concern). If they linger, a few teachers stop by with questions or congratulations — soft preview of Ch 2 faces, not a Meet the Teachers scene.
  4. Level 2 after the gremlins are defeated (book Ch 1 advance). Hand the level-up at table close if possible.
  5. First Study — between sessions: Ot (or faculty) points them at classes: pick primary + secondary branches, learn academia downtime, and craft their branch implement on the first Study. Point players at the Rules handouts; do not roll Study tonight.

Table risks and GM moves

  • Uzunjati sprawl: cap at 40 minutes. Moral question ends it. Ceremony needs the clock.
  • Printed fight too soft: run the pugwampi pad. Do not wait until round 3 to invent help.
  • Snare forgotten: place it; DC 18 Perception; Tiny ignore. First PC onto the marked zone learns.
  • Unluck Aura math: Will DC 20 with misfortune on the save itself — brutal for level 1. Telegraph bad luck comedy without soft-pedaling failed saves.
  • Kurshkin escape: scripted at <16 / fake surrender <10. Let her run if the table doesn’t pin her — Ch 3 wants the option.
  • Massacre mirror: if they butcher surrendering pugwampis, Ot still isn’t furious tonight — but Anchor / Mariama / thematic hangover is fair next session.
  • Over-schedule Ch 2: Meet Teachers after a Severe fight is how level-up joy dies. Hand Study as homework; resolve next session.
  • Implement / mask confusion: branch implement = first Study now. Spirit mask = First Masking later. Keep them separate when briefing players.
