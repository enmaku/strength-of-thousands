Open cold — Emerald briefing

Usual meeting place, morning after Cascade. Ignaci Canterells and Strands-of-Glowing-Dawn Tzeniwe wait with a basket of mail and silk sacks of overpacked supplies.

  1. Ignaci compliments any particularly well-dressed or good-looking heroes (at this table, that lands cleanest on Lithrei), then frames Emerald Boughs: study of communities and the present day — not “spies,” despite the rumor.
  2. Task: deliver the mail. “Not flashy, but vitally important.” Listening to recipients is part of the work.
  3. Tzeniwe: many stops are outside Nantambu → she rented elephant birds (one Large docile mount per PC; reins, no check).
  4. She unpacks snacks, coffee, umbrellas, towels, socks, muscle balm — then finds Zanvi’s web-covered toy elephant. Embarrassed mom beat; do not turn it into anadi lecture.
  5. Basket: two dozen letters and parcels, jumbled. Sorting and delivering is the day — for as long as it takes.

Tzeniwe’s portrait and presentation are human form. Twins Zachva & Zanvi shift freely. If spider stigma comes up, she is more forgiving of discomfort than her friends think she should be — do not punish PCs for surprise; reward courtesy. Hybrid-form newcomers (Goss/Mazta/Savana) are still chapters away.

Easy mail — two hours of city color

Most letters have clear addresses in or near Nantambu. Recipients are happy to talk. Play each as a 90-second vignette, not a skill gate — then move on. Dinner-hour drops sometimes get free meals; rainy season makes Tzeniwe’s towels diegetic, not fluff.

Table risks and GM moves

  • Sandbox sprawl: thirteen hard items can eat three hours. Batch easy color, then let the table pick which hard bundles to chase. Incomplete is OK.
  • Intimidation shortcut: telegraph that bullying Jatia makes Ignaci/Tzeniwe cold — Magaambya face in the neighborhood matters.
  • Steal / legal mess: crit fail ends the mail day. Have the consequence ready so you don’t soft-pedal.
  • Anadi reveal urge: do not force spider-form education. Zanvi’s web toy is enough texture.
  • Investigation detour: tunnels, Uduak, Koride wait. Emerald is the community day.
  • Over-scheduling: Uzunjati + ceremony after a full mail sandbox is how relationship beats die. Under-schedule.
