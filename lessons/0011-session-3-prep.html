Where Session 2 left you

Table canon Start at midday on the morning after the barn. The group has used its free morning; the Cascade assignment has not begun.

  • Tempest-Sun complete: six pugwampis left through the floor hatch without a fight. They admitted someone sent them to break things, but named no master.

  • Cy’An heard “tests do not eat.” They accepted Ot’s distinction between helping friends and taking away their chance to learn.
  • Solanum flew Haibram’s glider (~80 ft, safe landing) and is owed a formula. The dorm search also recovered unsettled handwraps of mighty blows.

Session thesis: Cascade’s virtue is imagination—but Magaambya magic is never free of consequence. Tonight the cohort invents a ward against gremlins while choosing how to treat living caterpillars, dead bones, and a predatory myceloid; then Mariama asks what else that clever ward might hurt. That is Book 1’s early scale of responsibility: campus service that already has ethical edges, foreshadowing later “curiosity’s shadow” when student solutions outrun wisdom.

Who needs it most tonight: Cy’An (help without erasing others’ learning—echo Ot’s “tests do not eat”), Yggostrus (respect living systems and Anchor’s expertise after the Session 2 rupture), Anya (protect the vulnerable without turning ethics into a speech). Open cold — Cascade briefing at midday

At the usual meeting place: Mariama Keitana, Anchor Root, and a sleeping Chizire. Preserve the book’s scene, but move it to midday to match Session 2 canon.

  1. Mariama admires Chizire’s ability to sleep anywhere.
  2. She frames Cascade Bearers as advancing magical theory and making new magic safe to use. Mention the “exploding nose incident”; refuse details.
  3. The barn proved gremlins are active, so the group will test a magical defense.
  4. Anchor lists scarlet cap, bone, and caterpillar oils. Attention makes her shrink; let her become confident when discussing ingredients.
  5. Mariama gives them eight hours: “Be done in eight hours, or you’ll miss dinner. And try to have fun!”
Anchor continuity

She is not back to zero. She likes most of the cohort and has already shown courage and expertise. With Yggostrus, she is polite but guarded. Do not force her to explain the insult again; a respectful task interaction is the better repair opportunity.

Umbo — dangerous, selfish, and uninterested in talk

  • Creature 3, weak myceloid; AC 18, 50 HP, slashing weakness 5.
  • Hidden in leaves above the mushrooms. When a PC climbs, he rides a broad leaf down and uses Emit Spores across as many targets as possible.
  • Purple pox: DC 18 Fortitude, 1-minute onset. Stage 1 deals 1d6 poison and stupefied 1 for a day. The later stages are lethal—make treatment urgency legible if anyone fails.
  • He wants humanoid flesh and will not negotiate. He flees into the jungle below 10 HP.
  • A climber automatically spots his cache: holly bush feather token, wolf fang, and hand of the mage.
Fair telegraph: the leaves move under weight before he drops; fungal growth pulses; the first spore cloud is visibly noxious. The group is healing-rich, but Umbo can still punish a clustered level-1 party.
Character use

Give Solanum an obvious slashing lane and let Esi’s battle-magic conversation echo through competence, not a flashback. If Lithrei’s passenger reacts to spores or domination, use one sensory flicker only. The fight belongs to the present task.

Fey abeyance — success is not the end of the lesson

Mariama adds cold-iron bells and explains that normal gremlin bells mostly work through superstition. This ritual creates a genuine ward in the barn.

RoleCheck
Primary casterTrained Nature, DC 21. Anchor takes the role if no PC qualifies.
Secondary casterDC 16 Crafting, Intimidation, Occultism, or Performance.
Cast1 hour. Ward lasts 1 week.
Critical failureNo effect; attracts troublesome fey at GM discretion. Keep any consequence short enough not to eclipse the session ending.

The ward makes fey uncomfortable, treats physical attacks against them as cold iron, and causes nonlethal mental damage in bell squares. After any result, Mariama notices the problem: it affects all fey, not only malicious gremlins.

GM-only consequence

Kurshkin later mistakes the barn clear and ward as the start of another anti-gremlin purge. Do not reveal that interpretation yet. Let the players leave believing they solved a real problem while understanding that protective systems can harm bystanders.

Table risks and GM moves

  • Analysis paralysis: put the eight-hour clock on the table. Advance it when plans consume meaningful time.
  • Party split: allow parallel sourcing, but keep most PCs on scarlet caps so Umbo remains playable and fair.
  • Umbo lethality: telegraph spores, remember his flight threshold, and surface disease treatment immediately after exposure.
  • Caterpillar ethics: the task requires killing them. Let discomfort exist; do not demand a philosophical defense from Anchor.
  • Yggostrus/Anchor repair: avoid an apology checkpoint. Trust should move through several respectful interactions.
  • Tunnel fixation: the school is responding. The Cascade ward is today’s work; tunnel exploration can wait.
  • Over-scheduling: Emerald is a new social sandbox. A clean ritual ending is better than rushing it.
