Branch Implements

Your Magaambya focus: a personal staff (or staff-like object) that grows with your primary branch, lets you practice blending traditions, and stays yours as you advance.

What a branch implement is

Magaambya magic is bigger than any one tradition — but blending other traditions has a cost until you truly master it. Your branch implement is a held focus that uses staff rules, grows with your primary branch, and lets you cast spells outside your usual lists at a higher charge cost.

A branch implement is a Magaambya-bonded magic item. It follows staff rules: you prepare it during daily preparations, spend charges to cast the spells inscribed on it, and use your own spell attack roll and spell DC.

The default form is a staff. You may choose another obvious held object within the limits below — the rules still treat it as your staff for the day.

It uses the Personal Staves idea that every custom staff is bound to a single trait, with Magaambya house rules for early capacity, off-list casting, an extra prepare charge, branch-tied upgrades, and a benefit while you hold it.

What this means at the Magaambya

  • The school studies and blends all four traditions — arcane, divine, occult, and primal. Branches emphasize different kinds of magic (traits and methods), not a single locked tradition.
  • Your Magaambyan Attendant and Halcyon Speaker feats still work as published. The implement is how you practice blending early; it does not replace those feats.

When you receive it

After your First Masking (when you become an attendant and receive your spirit mask), you choose your primary and secondary branches. Your first Study downtime then grants your branch implement for your primary branch, on top of the usual branch-level results of that Study.

  • Your secondary branch does not grant an implement.
  • You must already be able to cast spells. If you cannot cast spells yet, you receive the implement when you gain spellcasting.

Creating your implement

  1. State your primary branch and confirm that you can cast spells.
  2. Choose your form (default staff, or another legal held shape — see Form).
  3. Choose a binding trait from your branch’s allowed list (see Binding traits). This choice is permanent.
  4. Fill the Apprentice slots: 1 cantrip and 1 first-rank spell. Both must have your binding trait. They may come from any tradition. Prefer common spells; ask before taking uncommon ones.
  5. Name your implement. Magaambyans believe naming helps attunement.

The Magaambya supplies the spell castings to awaken the implement. There is no Craft cost and no gp cost. It is a school gift — not something you sell.

Form — staff rules, not only staff shape

  • Usage: held in 1 hand; Bulk 1; an obvious focus — not worn, not pocketable, not easily concealed.
  • Examples: wooden staff (default), orb, small statue, instrument, book, frying pan, a holy symbol large enough to occupy a hand, and similar.
  • As a weapon: it may use staff weapon statistics, or another agreed 1-hand simple weapon of Bulk 1 (such as a club). Weapon runes may be applied to the implement.
  • Disarmed or thrown: you cannot cast from it until you recover it.
  • Cosmetic restyle: free when the implement gains a tier. Changing its weapon die or weapon traits needs GM approval.

Binding traits by branch

Every spell on your implement must share one binding trait. Tradition traits (arcane, divine, occult, primal) and school traits are too broad — you cannot use those as the binding trait. Pick one allowed trait for your branch.

Cascade Bearers — Imagination

Suggested binding traits: Any trait (subject to GM approval). Cascade Bearers' versatility makes them a good fit for many traits — for example illusion, polymorph, light, force, an energy trait, or another narrow trait that fits your experimental style.

Emerald Boughs — Camaraderie

Suggested: mental, emotion.

People-magic, bonds, and community.

Rain-Scribes — Adaptability

Suggested: animal, plant, healing. If your concept is weather or terrain, you may also ask for water or air.

Wilderness, sustenance, and moving with the land.

Tempest-Sun Mages — Valor

Suggested: fire, electricity, air, force.

Offensive pressure and battlefield courage. (Many damaging spells are save-based and do not have the attack trait — that trait alone is a poor binder.)

Uzunjati — Knowledge

Suggested: mental, revelation, or detection (pick one).

Story, memory, and knowing. Linguistic or auditory spells can appear if they also have your binding trait.

Casting and charges

Unless noted here, use the published staff rules. Under the Remaster, preparing a staff gives free charges equal to your highest spell rank; prepared casters may also expend one spell slot to add more charges.

House rules for branch implements

RuleDetail
Always preparable You can prepare your branch implement even if none of its spells are on your spell list.
One staff per day Preparing the implement counts as preparing your staff for the day — even if it looks like a pan or orb. You cannot also prepare a second staff that day.
+1 Magaambya charge When you prepare the implement, it gains charges equal to your highest spell rank + 1.
On-list cost If the spell appears on any list you cast from (your class, a dedication, your Halcyon spells, and so on), charges equal the spell’s rank (cantrips cost 0).
Off-list cost Otherwise, charges equal the spell’s rank + 1 (cantrips cost 1). That extra charge is the cost of borrowing another tradition.
Spell rank You must still be able to cast spells of that rank. Higher ranks on the implement do nothing until your own spellcasting reaches them.
Attack and DC Use yours. As with other staves, the spell gains the trait for your magical tradition when you cast it.

Examples (daily prepare, no extra slot spent)

  • Levels 1–2 (highest rank 1 → 2 charges): one off-list cantrip (1 charge), or one on-list 1st-rank spell (1). An off-list 1st costs 2 — you can cast it once, with nothing left.
  • Levels 3–4 (highest rank 2 → 3 charges): for example one off-list 1st (2) and one off-list cantrip (1), or several cheaper on-list options.
  • Prepared casters can expend a spell slot while preparing to add more charges (as normal for staves). Spending a 1st-rank slot at level 1 can raise you to 2 charges and unlock a single off-list 1st — a real trade for that day.

Upgrades (primary branch level)

Your implement grows when your primary branch level reaches the thresholds below (after Study, Cram, or Practical Research finishes resolving, including when a ★ is spent because your character level caught up). Your secondary branch does not upgrade it.

You must still be able to cast a given rank to use those spells from the implement.

At Attuned (primary branch 3) you gain a second cantrip slot. Apprentice keeps one cantrip; every later tier keeps two. (Published Personal Staves stay at one cantrip forever — this is a Magaambya exception.)

When your implement gains a tier

  • You unlock the new rank slots (and at Attuned, a second cantrip). Spells you already chose stay.
  • You choose new spells for empty slots (same binding trait). The Magaambya supplies the castings — no Craft cost or gp.
  • Retrain: you may swap one existing spell for another legal spell of the same rank.
  • Your binding trait never changes.

If it is lost or destroyed

The Magaambya replaces it at your current tier after appropriate downtime or recovery. Only you can prepare it.

Benefits while holding

These apply while you hold the implement in hand (including the hand you use to cast from it).

Unless noted otherwise, the circumstance bonuses below use your primary branch level: +1 at levels 0–10, +2 at 11–16, and +3 at 17 or higher.

Cascade Bearers

You gain this circumstance bonus to checks to Identify Magic or Recall Knowledge about magical phenomena.

Emerald Boughs

You gain this circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks to Make an Impression or Request, and to Society checks to Recall Knowledge about settlements and cultures you have visited.

Rain-Scribes

You gain this circumstance bonus to Survival checks to Sense Direction, Subsist, or Track. You also ignore the first square of non-magical difficult terrain you enter each time you Stride (this does not scale).

Tempest-Sun Mages

You gain this circumstance bonus to your Fortitude and Will DCs against attempts to Disarm you of the implement or Shove you, and to Intimidation checks to Demoralize.

Uzunjati

You gain this circumstance bonus to Recall Knowledge checks, and to Performance checks when telling a story or orating (including Uzunjati Storytelling, once you have that feat).

Branch implements and Halcyon magic

As you rise in the Magaambya you may take Magaambyan Attendant and later Halcyon Speaker feats. Those feats are separate from your implement and work as published.

  • The implement is an early way to practice spells from other traditions (with the off-list charge tax).
  • Halcyon Speaker and related feats are how you claim deeper, lasting Halcyon spellcasting.
  • If a spell on your implement later appears on your lists (from your class, a dedication, Halcyon spells, and so on), casting it from the implement drops to the normal on-list charge cost.
Pathbuilder — tracking your branch implement

Pathbuilder does not have a first-class “custom Magaambya staff” type. The usual workaround is three pieces: a generic caster for the inscribed spells, a custom buff to count charges, and a custom gear item for the physical implement. Pathbuilder’s UI moves around between versions — look for the same labels if a menu sits somewhere slightly different.

1. Enable staff spells (generic caster)

  1. Open your character and go to Character Options.
  2. Enable Add generic caster for staves etc. (sometimes labeled Add generic caster).
  3. That adds an extra spell tab on your sheet for staff / wand spells.
  4. On that tab, add each spell inscribed on your implement and set how many times it appears at each rank (match your current tier from the upgrade table above).
Pathbuilder will not enforce Magaambya house rules for you. Remember: you can prepare the implement even if none of its spells are on your list; free charges are highest spell rank + 1; off-list casts cost rank + 1 (cantrips cost 1).

2. Track charges with a custom buff

  1. Open Buffs (custom effects).
  2. Create a new custom buff named something simple like Staff Charges or your implement’s name.
  3. Leave mechanical bonuses blank — this is only a counter.
  4. During play, use the buff’s + / − controls at the top of your sheet to spend and restore charges after daily preparations.

3. Held benefits that are flat bonuses

Some benefits while holding are unconditional circumstance bonuses (they apply whenever the implement is in hand, not only on a named action). Those can be separate custom buffs with the bonus filled in — turn the buff on when you hold the implement and off when you don’t. Leave fiddlier riders (specific actions only, ignore one square of terrain, and so on) as notes on the gear item instead.

4. Add the physical item

  1. On the Gear, Weapons, or Offense tab, create a custom item for the implement.
  2. Write in its name, Bulk 1, held in 1 hand, binding trait, and a short note of Magaambya house rules (or paste a link to this page).
  3. If you use it as a weapon, set staff (or your agreed simple weapon) statistics and any weapon runes there.

If Pathbuilder’s menus don’t match these labels exactly, search Character Options for “generic caster” / “staves” — the feature name has varied slightly between versions.
