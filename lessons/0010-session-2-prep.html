Pace estimate for Session 2

Session 1 burned most of an evening on orientation + one social-heavy Perquisite day (six players, market deep-dive). Tonight opens with a capped dorm beat, then book density jumps: day 2 is a Severe-adjacent skill/combat puzzle; day 3 is a timed three-fetch + Creature 3 ambush + ritual.

BandPlan
Expected Spire dorm (Anchor + Haibram, ≤20 min) → Tempest-Sun (barn) → Cascade Bearers (oils + Umbo + ritual) if time.
Stretch Open Emerald Boughs mail (Ignaci + Tzeniwe) if Cascade finishes with ≥45 minutes left. Do not force completion.
Cut early If dorm + Tempest run long (Uduak research, bells, Anchor diplomacy, hatch talk): end after day 2. Cascade becomes Session 3 open.
Do not bank on Uzunjati storytelling or Introduction Ceremony tonight. Ceremony is a Severe 1 climax — deserves oxygen, not a rush after two full days.
Matching book pace: The module budgets ~1 week for all five Perquisite days + ceremony. Your table is slower and richer — that is fine. Keep emotional pace (cohort faces → service → nonlethal ethic → Uduak seed) even if calendar days spill across more sessions. Do not turn the dorm open into a second Kafesi crawl.

Open — Spire dorm (Anchor + Haibram)

Keep book order (Tempest → Cascade). Use a short dorm scene so Anchor is a person before she is a barn tool, and Haibram gets a real sky beat (market day was task-focused). Solanum (reads the sky) and Taraan (obsessed with learning to fly) both lean toward Haibram — share the moment, don’t make it a duel for the NPC.

Hard cap: ~15–20 minutes · two faces only (Anchor + Haibram). Esi can hover / arrive to collect them for Tempest — that is the exit ramp. Save Mariama, Tzeniwe, Ignaci, Okoro, Noxolo for their branch days.

Uduak seed load-bearing

Esi: a student was expelled for killing gremlins “because of who they are.” She knows broad strokes only; points curious PCs to teachers or records after the job. Library research on pugwampis also surfaces Uduak Basni’s story up to his presumed death.

GM only — do not say tonight

Uduak is the future Stone Ghost. This scene seeds the Magaambya ethic: don’t slaughter pests for existing. Moral mirror for the whole party’s lethal toolkit (breath, Explode, eidolon teeth) — not Cy’An’s personal growth spine. Soft optional rhyme for Cy’An: “permanent fix” as over-providing. Reward clever nonlethal / scare / Anchor Root paths with full combat XP.

Day 3 — Cascade Bearers (oils + ritual)

Framing

Three fetches (parallelize)

OilHowWatch-outs

Caterpillar Fuzzy jungle caterpillars → oil (book details in extract). Time sink; keep moving. Don’t let perfect-three-oils perfectionism eat Emerald stretch.

Ritual & aftermath

Mariama directs the ritual whether it succeeds or fails. She notes it may harm any fey, not just gremlins — Cascade caution theme. XP: up to 90 for oils + 80/120 for ritual attempt/success.

Ceremony foreshadow

Later, Kurshkin mistakes the barn clear + fey-abeyance ritual as the start of another purge — motivation for the Introduction Ceremony ambush. You don’t need to telegraph that tonight.

Table risks & GM moves

  • Dorm crawl time sink — Session 1 market energy wants to meet everyone. Cap at Anchor + Haibram; Esi’s arrival ends the scene. If they chase Noxolo/Tzeniwe, “out on errands — you’ll see them on branch day.”
  • Lethal AoE (Cy’An Explode, Yggostrus breath) vs nonlethal mandate — table risk for the whole party; let Esi’s line land; Magaambya holds students accountable (That’s Going on Your Permanent Record sidebar). Do not treat this as Cy’An’s character thesis — their Book 1 drip is provider-as-identity (see tie-ins).
  • Umbo is Creature 3 vs level 1 — Moderate/Severe feel. Spores punish clustered melee. Allow flee to campus for help without shame.
  • Weak party Reflex — pugwampi chaos and spore clouds both lean here; telegraph misfortune aura.
  • Time sink: perfect Kafesi-style investigation on Cascade fetches. Time-box: “dinner bell in X hours” diegetically.
  • Hatch curiosity: note + defer. “Smells wrong / goes deep” is enough.
