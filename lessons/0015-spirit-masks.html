Spirit Masks

Your Magaambyan animal mask: how you make it, what it does at first, and the three as-written paths that give it real sheet powers later.

What a spirit mask is

Your spirit mask is a personal wooden animal face that holds a fragment of your spirit and shows your spirit face to the Magaambya. At First Masking it is identity and belonging — not an automatic magic item. Powers come later through familiar feats, transferring found mask enchantments, or crafting.

Attendants earn the right to wear a Magaambyan mask (or to have a personal mask acknowledged by the school). Tradition carves it as an animal. Continued wear gradually imprints your true spiritual face into the wood.

Wearing it is traditional and meaningful; it is not mandatory every hour of every day. Some students find moving or speaking masks unsettling and leave theirs off outside ceremony.

Sources: Strength of Thousands Player’s Guide · Kindled Magic Magical Masks (p. 73) · AoN — Mask Familiar · AoN — Magaambyan Attendant (Pathbuilder: Collegiate Attendant).

Making yours

You craft your mask for the First Masking ceremony in Tireless Hall — the rite that marks initiate → attendant. Typical process:

  1. Choose an animal that reflects your spirit face (how you want the Magaambya to know you). You will be associated with that appearance for the rest of your career here.
  2. Carve or finish a wooden mask in that form. Materials and tools are available on campus for this ceremony; it is not a full Campus Crafting project unless the GM says otherwise.
  3. Wear it for the ceremony. The school acknowledges the mask as yours.

Pick with care: later Magaambya traditions (including very high-level options) lean on the animal you chose. You can reflavor published options to fit an unusual animal if the spirit matches.

What it does at first

  • It is a mundane worn mask (Bulk —, or Bulk 1 if a particular published full-head design says so later).
  • It has no invested activations, bonuses, or item level until something from Gaining powers applies.
  • It still matters socially and narratively: fellow students and teachers recognize attendants by their masks; your animal is part of how you are known.

Three paths to powers

These are separate routes. You can combine some of them over a career, but each has its own gates.

Path What you need What you get
Familiar Magaambyan Attendant dedication, then Mask Familiar Your mask becomes a familiar (wear or detach as colored light)
Transfer A found magical mask + teacher help + 1 day + still-mundane personal mask Your spirit mask gains that item’s abilities
Craft Normal magic-item Crafting (Campus Crafting at this table) You enchant your personal mask (or craft a published mask design as yours)

Familiar

After you have Magaambyan Attendant Dedication (often as a branch bonus feat once you are an attendant with branch rank 3), you may take Mask Familiar (feat 4).

  • Your mask takes on a personality and serves as your familiar.
  • You can still wear it. Detaching or reattaching it to transform between mask and bodily form is a two-action activity. Detached, it is usually a miniature of the animal in colored light.
  • Even while worn, it can move on your face and speak in its own voice (if it can talk). Unless an attempt to hide the mask's true nature is made, it is obviously more than a simple mask.
  • It gains the special familiar ability Mask Freeze: in mask form it can pass as an ordinary mask at a glance, and gains a +4 circumstance bonus to its Deception DC against someone actively Seeking or studying it.
  • Otherwise it follows normal familiar rules.

Adaptive Mask Familiar (requires Mask Familiar) lets you select four master and familiar abilities each day instead of two.

Transfer

During the adventure you may find magical masks as treasure (the Magical Masks toolbox lists designs such as goz, herd, mercy, rhinoceros, shapespeak, skittering, and others).

For certain finds, a Magaambya teacher (often Takulu Ot) can help transfer that mask’s abilities onto a non-magical mask you already own — including the mask from your First Masking. This takes 1 day of downtime.

As written, the destination must be non-magical. Transfer is “put these abilities onto my blank spirit mask,” not “stack another enchantment onto an already-magic mask.”
  • After a successful transfer, your personal mask is that magic item (same activations, investiture, Bulk, and so on), still looking like your animal.
  • You may instead wear the found mask as-is and leave your spirit mask mundane. That is legal; you just are not combining faces.
  • If your spirit mask is already magical, ask the GM before assuming a second transfer or upgrade — the published transfer note does not cover that case.

Craft / enchant

Magaambyans often place useful enchantments on their masks for dangerous work. As written, that uses normal magic-item crafting:

  • Meet the usual gates (item level, access/formula, tools, Magical Crafting for magic items).
  • At this table, use Campus Crafting for time and materials when you are making or enchanting gear for the party.
  • You can craft a published mask item (e.g. a goz mask) and treat it as your personal Magaambyan mask, or enchant your existing wooden mask into one of those designs when the GM agrees the formula fits.

Crafting after a transfer, or transferring onto a still-mundane mask before you craft, are both finer than the books spell out in detail — agree on the end state with the GM so Pathbuilder and treasure stay honest.

Published mask examples: Skittering Mask · Herd Mask · Magical Masks in Kindled Magic / Treasure Vault. Party craft procedure: Campus Crafting on the Rules tab.

Later options (pointer only)

Much later in Magaambya careers, high-level options themed to the Magic Warriors may unlock further mask-based feats keyed to the animal you chose at First Masking. Treat those as future unlocks when the campaign reaches them — they are not part of First Masking or early attendant play.

Pathbuilder — tracking your spirit mask

Pathbuilder has no “Magaambya spirit mask” type. Track the physical mask as gear, then wire feats and familiars through the same menus used for other Magaambya bonus options. Labels drift by platform and version — if a name below is missing, search the ☰ menu for the closest match.

1. Mundane spirit mask (custom gear)

  1. Open your character and go to the Inventory / Gear tab (sometimes labeled Equipment).
  2. Tap Add Item (or the + control), then choose Custom Item / Create Custom Item — not a published catalog entry yet.
  3. Set Name to something clear, e.g. Spirit Mask — buffalo.
  4. Set Usage / worn slot to worn mask (or “worn” + a note that it is a mask). Bulk — unless a later magic version says otherwise.
  5. In the item’s description / notes, write the animal, that it is your First Masking spirit mask, and that it is still mundane.
  6. Leave attack, AC, and bonus fields empty. Do not mark it invested until it becomes a magic item.

2. After transfer or craft (magic item)

If the powers match a published mask Pathbuilder knows (goz, skittering, herd, mercy, rhinoceros, shapespeak, and so on):

  1. On Inventory / Gear, tap Add Item → search the equipment catalog for the published mask name.
  2. Add that item. Open it and edit the name or notes so it is clear this is your spirit mask (same animal face) — e.g. rename to Spirit Mask — buffalo (Mask of Mercy).
  3. Mark it invested if the item is Invested (Pathbuilder usually has an Invest toggle on worn magic items).
  4. Delete or unequip your old mundane custom mask entry so you do not track two masks.
  5. If you previously listed the found loot mask separately, remove that entry too — its powers now live on your spirit mask.

If Pathbuilder has no matching item (or the GM gave a custom activation):

  1. Open your existing custom spirit-mask item on Inventory / Gear and edit it.
  2. Paste the full activation text into description / notes. Set level, Price, Bulk, and Invested to match the item.
  3. If the item grants a flat bonus Pathbuilder can model, add it on the item or as a custom buff (☰ → Buffs / custom effects) that you toggle when the mask is worn. Leave triggers and once-per-day riders in the notes.
Pathbuilder will not enforce “destination must be non-magical” for transfer. Confirm with the GM before you replace the mundane entry.

3. Collegiate Attendant Dedication (Magaambyan Attendant)

Pathbuilder cannot ship “Magaambya” product-identity names. Search for Collegiate Attendant Dedication — same feat, licensing rename — then choose your branch when prompted.

If the dedication is a branch bonus feat (Dedicated Attendant at branch rank 3 — does not spend a class feat):

  1. Open the menu (☰) and choose Custom Feat Choices (sometimes near Custom Pack / optional rules; wording may be “Custom Feat” / “Feat Choices”).
  2. Add a new choice of type archetype feat / dedication (whatever label Pathbuilder offers for dedications).
  3. Search for and select Collegiate Attendant Dedication.
  4. When Pathbuilder asks for branch affiliation, pick your primary Magaambya branch.
  5. If it warns that you have not finished another archetype yet, ignore it for this campaign: Dedicated Attendant grants the dedication even without two prior archetype feats.

If you are taking the dedication with a normal class / archetype feat slot instead:

  1. Open the Feats tab (or level-up flow for the level that grants the class feat).
  2. Choose an archetype / dedication feat slot, search Collegiate Attendant Dedication, select it, and set branch affiliation.

Either way, do not also spend a level-up feat on it if it already came free from your branch.

4. Mask Familiar (and Adaptive Mask Familiar)

Bonus / free archetype feat (rare for Mask Familiar, but use this if the GM grants it outside a level-up):

  1. ☰ → Custom Feat Choices.
  2. Add an archetype feat choice.
  3. Search for Mask Familiar (under Collegiate / Magaambyan Attendant). Select it.
  4. For Adaptive Mask Familiar later, repeat with that feat name.

Normal archetype feat at a level-up (usual case):

  1. On the Feats tab (or when leveling), open the class / archetype feat picker for that level.
  2. Filter or search archetype feats for Mask Familiar. You need Collegiate Attendant Dedication on the sheet first or Pathbuilder may hide the feat.
  3. Select Mask Familiar. Later, take Adaptive Mask Familiar the same way when you qualify.

5. Configure the familiar on the sheet

  1. Open the menu (☰) and look for Familiars & Pets, Familiar, or a Pets tab (exact label varies; some builds put this under Character Options).
  2. Add / enable a familiar. Name it after your mask animal if you like.
  3. In the familiar’s notes or description, write that it is your spirit mask: wear form vs colored-light form, and that detach / reattach is a two-action activity (Pathbuilder will not enforce that).
  4. Pick your daily familiar and master abilities as usual on that screen.
  5. Mask Freeze is not always a catalog familiar ability. If it is not in the picker, add a line in the familiar notes: Mask Freeze — passes as a simple mask at a glance; +4 circumstance to Deception DC when someone actively Seeks / studies it. You can also put the same reminder on the gear item notes.
  6. If you take Adaptive Mask Familiar, remember you select four abilities per day — Pathbuilder may still show a two-ability UI; track the extras in notes or ask the GM how your table marks them.

If a menu label does not match exactly, search ☰ for “Custom Feat,” “Familiar,” or “Inventory” — Pathbuilder’s wording varies slightly by platform and version. Academia Downtime on the Rules tab has the same Custom Feat Choices walkthrough for other branch bonus feats.
