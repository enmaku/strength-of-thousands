Campus Crafting

How Magaambya students make gear between classes: faster than published Craft, cheaper on a good check, and built for a handful of useful items in one short downtime.

What Campus Crafting is

Crafting at this table is about making things you will use during short Magaambya downtimes — not grinding the Earn Income table for weeks to shave a few gold off an item. Time scales with how simple the job is relative to you; the Crafting check sets how much of the Price you keep paying.

Campus Crafting replaces the published Craft activity whenever you create an item for yourself or your allies. It keeps Remaster requirements (level, feats, tools, access) but changes setup time, material cost, and how a check resolves.

Want pocket money instead of gear? Use Earn Income with Crafting (or another skill). That is a different activity.

Baseline: AoN — Craft · AoN — Item Crafting. This page is the Magaambya house rule.

When to use it

  • Use Campus Crafting to make or upgrade gear, consumables, or tools your party will use.
  • Use Earn Income (Crafting) to sell labor or finished goods for gold.
  • Campus Crafting is downtime only — hours or days between adventures, not during combat or rushed exploration. Repair and other exploration Craft uses stay as published.

Requirements

Same gates as published Craft unless noted:

  • The item’s level is your level or lower. Items with no listed level are level 0. Items of 9th level or higher require master Crafting; 17th or higher require legendary.
  • The item is common, or you otherwise have access (including a formula for uncommon or rarer items).
  • You have appropriate tools and, when needed, a workshop.
  • Alchemical Crafting for alchemical items; Magical Crafting for magic items.

A formula is not required for common items. Having a formula still speeds crafting when the time table says so.

Procedure

  1. Confirm you meet the requirements and have a legal formula or access if needed.
  2. Spend materials equal to 25% of the item’s Price (see Materials). School supplies may cover some or all of this (see School supplies).
  3. Spend the craft time from the Time table. A working day is 8 hours; you may sequence several shorter jobs in one downtime.
  4. Attempt a Crafting check against the DC in Crafting DC.
  5. Apply the check result: finish the item or not, and settle materials.

Consumables still craft in batches of up to four of the same item with one check (pay materials for the whole batch). Non-magical ammunition still uses the batch size on the weapons table.

Crafting DC

Your Crafting DC is the level-based DC for the item’s level, plus a rarity adjustment if the item is not common. Items with no listed level use level 0.

Other adjustments

  • If you have successfully Campus Crafted this exact item before, the GM may apply the easy adjustment (−2).
  • Other circumstance adjustments are rare; the GM will say when one applies.

Example: a common 3rd-level item is DC 18. An uncommon 3rd-level item is DC 20 (18 + 2).

Published rule: use the item’s level for the DC, then apply rarity (and optional easy if you’ve made it before). AoN — Level-Based DCs · AoN — Craft (DCs).

Materials

Front materials worth 25% of the Price (for a batch, 25% of the batch’s total Price). After the check:

CheckResultNet materials cost
Critical success Item complete. Refund materials down to 10% of Price. 10%
Success Item complete. 25%
Failure No item. Full refund of materials. Time is spent. 0%
Critical failure No item. Lose materials equal to 10% of Price; refund the rest. Time is spent. 10%
There is no second half of the Price to grind down with Earn Income days. When the check succeeds, you are done — you already paid the net cost above.

  • Minimum craft time is still 1 hour (no in-combat crafting).
  • Interrupted work can resume later from where you left off if the project is safely stored.
  • One downtime stretch can cover several items if the hours add up — a handful of simple tools or kits in a free week is expected.

School supplies

During a Magaambya Study or Practical Research downtime (in addition to that activity’s usual results), the school will cover Campus Crafting materials for common items of your level or lower, up to a budget of your level × 2 gp for that downtime.

  • Unused budget does not roll over.
  • Uncommon or rarer items, and anything above the budget, come from your own coin or materials.
  • This stipend is for Campus Crafting only — it is not free shopping credit.

Limits

  • Party use: Campus Crafting makes gear for you and your allies. It is not a market engine. Selling what you made uses normal sell rules (typically half Price) and should not outpace Earn Income as a reason to Craft.
  • Branch implements: your Magaambya staff-like focus is a school gift with its own rules — it has no Craft cost and does not use this activity.
  • Treasure: Campus Crafting does not raise the party’s expected magic-item wealth above what the adventure already allows. At-level permanent magic still takes days and Magical Crafting; it is not free loot.
Pathbuilder — tracking crafted gear

Pathbuilder does not simulate Campus Crafting. Add finished items to your sheet the same way you would add purchased gear.

  1. When an item is complete, add it under Gear, Weapons, Armor, or consumables as appropriate.
  2. Subtract the net materials cost (10% or 25% of Price, or school stipend) from your tracked money — Pathbuilder will not do the house-rule math for you.
  3. For a work-in-progress across sessions, keep a short note on the character (or a custom item named “WIP: …”) with hours already spent and materials already fronted.
