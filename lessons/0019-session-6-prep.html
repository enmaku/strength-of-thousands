Session thesis

Session thesis: a branch is a promise about how you will serve, and the first people it owes something to are the students beside you. Whoever still needs a sheet names that promise in public; then the cohort spends the evening on a lost familiar, a glider, and one classmate’s small crisis, and each favor asks whether the virtue they claimed shows up.

Who needs it most: Anya (she missed the briefing and still has to choose in public), Cy’An (the rolling pin has to be a Magaambya vow, not only a joke), Yggostrus (a branch is stewardship, not another private power). Thematic guide: Word and Way — study unused is incomplete — opening Book 1’s semester arc, becoming someone the school recognizes.

Catch up the unfinished sheets

Say this before anyone opens a book: the fight is over, Ot already handled the morning, you are level 2 initiates, masks come later. People who already chose a branch and built an implement do not rebuild them. Roll Study if that check has not happened yet, write the result, and go to the favors.

Have the three handouts open for whoever is still deciding: Branches, Branch Implements, Academia Downtime. Do their rounds while the early arrivals listen. Do not restart the spell argument for a finished implement.

  1. Branches, one pass. Each PC names a primary and a secondary. They must differ. Both start at 0. They may still change until the Study roll. Ask which virtue they are willing to be answerable for — imagination, camaraderie, adaptability, valor, knowledge — not which feat is strongest. Anya’s reliable Study skills are Nature, Survival, and Intimidation; if she stalls, offer Rain-Scribes or Tempest-Sun as the rolls she can actually make. Do not assign anyone a branch.
  2. Form, trait, name. Default form is a staff: one hand, Bulk 1, obvious, not worn or pocketed. Other legal shapes (orb, book, pan, holy symbol that fills a hand) use staff rules, or an agreed one-hand simple weapon of Bulk 1. Cy’An’s rolling pin is already declared and legal. Binding trait comes from the primary branch’s list and is permanent — say that before they lock it. Then they name it.
  3. Apprentice spells. One cantrip and one 1st-rank spell. Both must have the binding trait. Any tradition. Common by default; uncommon needs a yes from you. Timebox this round. Sheet debugging (Solanum’s missing Force Fang, Pathbuilder tabs) waits for a break.
  4. Then the Study roll in the next section. The implement is the school’s gift when that roll resolves. No Craft check and no gp.
Keep separate

Branch implement = this Study. Spirit mask = First Masking, still later. If someone reaches for the mask handout, point them back at the implement page.

Implements: first Study grants Apprentice (1 cantrip + 1 rank-1). Holding benefit is already +1 at primary branch 0–10. At level 2, preparing it gives charges equal to highest spell rank + 1, so 2 charges. On-list cantrips cost 0; off-list cantrips cost 1; an off-list 1st costs 2 (one cast, then empty).

First Study — roll it here

One downtime block, about a semester, resolved in one check. Each PC picks either primary or secondary and rolls one associated skill against the standard DC of the branch’s current level. Everyone is at branch 0, so the DC is 14.

ResultBranchAlso
Critical success+2 levelsImplement still granted
Success+1 levelImplement still granted
FailureNo changeImplement still granted. The gift is for sitting the class, not for acing it.
Critical failureNo changeImplement still granted. They skip the next Study (remedial work).

Caps at character level 2

  • Primary cannot pass character level: cap 2. A crit from 0 lands on 2 exactly (Additional Lore at 1, Steeped in History +1 at 2). No star.
  • Secondary cannot pass half character level: cap 1. Success lands on 1. A crit tries to reach 2, so they stop at 1 and mark a ★. That star spends when half their level becomes 2 — character level 4, not level 3.
  • Cram is not available. Cram needs both branches 2 or more below their caps, and the secondary is only 1 below. Say so if someone asks. One class this term.
  • Anchor’s advantage: Rain-Scribe Study critical failures become failures (the Session 3 caterpillar cutting). Use it if anyone Studies Rain-Scribes.

Write the result before you stand up: branch chosen, new level, lore or bonus gained, implement name, trait, two spells. A failure is a real semester. Do not offer a reroll to be kind.

Classmate favors — two or three

Run them in this order. Each is one or two checks and 30 XP if they pull it off. Cap a scene when the check resolves and the student says thank you. The cohort can split who rolls; they do not need a full-table investigation.

1. Lost Chicken expected

Anchor’s familiar is on the dormitory roof, frantic, after wandering too close to the Soaring Spire. She is too upset to search well. DC 15 Perception to spot it, DC 15 Athletics to bring it down. She squeaks a thanks and disappears into her room. One line, if you want a wink: centipedes have been in the dorm walls. Do not hand them Koride’s list. This is Anya’s beat if she is here; anyone else can roll the Athletics.

2. Lofty Leap expected

Same spire, next. Haibram’s new compact glider needs more height than the spire’s usual impossible leap. He asks Solanum — the plans already went through her — to balance at the peak with fingers interlocked so he can kick off another 60 feet. DC 15 Acrobatics to Balance, then DC 15 Athletics to hurl him. Both successes: the glider works, and he insists he owes her. Taraan may help from the ground. He does not take the ask away from her. A failure is a bruised dwarf and a “we’ll try again,” not a rescue scene.

3. One evening favor expected

Pick one. Save the other for the stretch.

  • Okoro’s board game (default; Taraan). A new game with miserable rules, ahead of the biweekly night. DC 14 Occultism or Society to decipher them. A crit on that check finds a cipher page: a scroll of augury, and he lets them keep it. Playing is DC 18 Occultism or Society; anyone who failed to learn the rules worsens that result by one step. Multiple winners are fine. No Ird, no tunnels, no Janatimo.
  • Tzeniwe’s party (if Cy’An or Lithrei are driving). Zachva and Zanvi want a surprise birthday for their mother and will not stop “helping.” DC 13 Diplomacy convinces Esi or Noxolo to mind them, or DC 17 Deception distracts the twins. If the heroes get a minute to plan, the party lands and Tzeniwe is genuinely grateful. DC 15 Perception to Sense Motive: she was not surprised, and she is pretending for the children. Noxolo here is a favor, not another reading.

  • Cricket theft only if they are still game. Chizire wants rare crickets from Koride’s terrarium for a brew. Table risks and GM moves

  • Rebuilding finished sheets: early arrivals keep what they already chose. Catch up the people who were not there. If a late spell choice stalls, default the form to a staff, lock the trait, and finish spells at the break.
  • Favor sprawl: two checks and a thank-you, then the next student. Three favors is a full evening. Four is the stretch, not the plan. Lost Chicken does not become a centipede hunt.
  • Cram and cap mistakes: one Study, DC 14, secondary cap 1, no Cram. A secondary crit banks a star until level 4. A bad roll still gets the implement.
  • Implement vs mask: say it again when someone asks about carving. Masks are the ceremony you are not running.
  • Anya catching up: she should not spend the night reconstructing Session 5. Two sentences, then the same rounds as everyone else.
  • Sheet tech: Force Fang and Pathbuilder exports are a break problem. They ate the end of Session 5. They do not get the end of this one.
  • Reopening the morning: the recap is closed. A player question gets one sentence, then the next favor. Ot does not hand over Koride’s list or the missing books. A caught cricket theft does not become that list either.
  • Identity spoilers: Stone Ghost stays a name. Uduak stays a dead student in a ledger. Kurshkin stays alive.
  • Six-player kindness: crits on DC 14 will happen. Branch 2 on a primary crit is the reward. Do not soften a crit fail into a success; remedial Study is the lesson that class is real.
