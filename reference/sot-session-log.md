# Session log — Strength of Thousands

Living campaign bookmark for writing the next **Sessions** GM prep lesson. Update after every session (or while drafting the next prep). Spoilers throughout.

## How to use

1. Read **Where we are** + last session’s **Covered / Not covered**.
2. Estimate next session from table pace (see `sot-session-prep-guide.md`).
3. Write `lessons/00NN-session-N-prep.html` and add it under **Sessions** in `src/domain/gmLessons.js`.
4. After the session runs, move estimate → actual coverage here before drafting N+1.

### Session 2

**Covered**

- Perquisite day 2 — **Tempest-Sun**: the party recruited **Anchor Root**, spoke with six pugwampis, and cleared the barn without combat or deaths. Three fled after Anya smashed a jar; Anchor sternly ordered the other three into the tunnels.
- The pugwampis admitted they had been **sent to break things**, but would not identify their master.
- The party discovered extensive recent tunnels beneath the barn and Spire Dorm. Taraan’s familiar later mapped part of the network; known surface access points are the barn hatch and a dorm passage. Chizire has a hidden card room nearby.
- The group pursued the mystery through Esi, Chizire, Takulu Ot, records, and tunnel exploration. They learned **Uduak** was a violent earth mage expelled two or three years ago for murdering gremlins during his Perquisite.
- **Noxolo** completed Ot’s feather test with uncontrolled fear magic, frightened the summoned bird, and fled crying. Ot stressed that she is at school to learn control.
- Ot privately gave **Cy’An** the “tests do not eat” lesson. Cy’An accepted the point and connected their instinct to caring for younger siblings.
- Free-time scenes: Solanum bonded with Esi over battle magic; Solanum flew Haibram’s experimental glider (~80 ft, safe landing) and was promised a glider formula; Lithrei researched the red-star/gateway omen; Yggostrus asked Anchor about Rain-Scribes, the cycle of life and death, and Kholo bonekeeping (then damaged trust).
- Dorm search found **spellskein** frogs (now in the pond) and **handwraps of mighty blows** in abandoned laundry; ownership unsettled.
- Open loops left on the table: Uduak’s former Spire room (near/possibly Yggostrus’s), Esi’s unread catfolk-biology book for Yggostrus, Chizire’s illness-faking mold, Haibram’s glider formula, Cy’An’s comfort food for Noxolo / beetle treats for Anchor.

### Session 3

**Covered**

- Recap of Session 2 (tunnels, Uduak, pugwampis, Ot/Cy’An).
- Perquisite day 3 — **Cascade Bearers**: **Mariama Keitana** briefing (**Chizire** still asleep in the forest; **Anchor Root** filling in); eight-hour clock for three oils (lucky bones, scarlet-cap mushrooms, caterpillars).
- **Scarlet-cap trek**: Anya’s cat climbs for mushrooms; **Umbo** (myceloid) appears. Fortitude DC 18 — Yggostrus (17), Anya (15), and Yggostrus’s eidolon (12) fail → **purple pox**; Cy’An passes (20).
- **First PF2e combat** vs Umbo. Initiative: Lithrei 24, Cy’An 23, Yggostrus 19, Solanum 15, Anya 6. Lithrei used Intimidation; Cy’An named **MODU** (Mobile Oven Defense Unit); Solanum tried Administer First Aid on dying Umbo (failed). Umbo killed by coup de grâce (spore-burst concern); **skull-shaped Perquisite bead** on Yggostrus’s bracelet. Solanum distressed about myceloid death (leshy solidarity).
- **Oils gathering**: caterpillars (gloves); lucky bones from butcher (Anya Intimidation); purple pox treated at campus first aid (Anya and Yggostrus cured). Wolf fang talisman loot.
- **Fey-abeyance**: all three oils crafted with group Aid; Chizire never needed. Lithrei gave Anchor a caterpillar-plant cutting → **Rain-Scribe classroom advantage** (crit fail → fail on Rain-Scribe Study). **Fey-abeyance ritual** on cold-iron bells succeeded (Yggostrus primary Nature). Mariama: ward affects **any fey**, not just gremlins — party will tell **Teacher Ot**. Mariama lives in Spire Dorm (revealed).
- **Aftermath free time**: Lithrei Occultism on **Noxolo** — sense of ambient darkness; **Koride Ulawa** named as Rain-Scribe teacher. Yggostrus apologized to Anchor: personal forgiveness (“we’re fine”) but **“on behalf of my people, apology not accepted”** — repair through action (“pick up a shovel”). Cy’An keeping wolf fang for Anya gift. Taraan continued tunnel mapping (confusing network).
